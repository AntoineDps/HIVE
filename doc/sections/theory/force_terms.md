# Force Terms

This page defines each force term that appears in the equation of motion, how it is computed by HIVE, and which column in the processed CSV it maps to.

---

## Excitation force — `fe`

The excitation force is the total hydrodynamic force on the body due to the incident wave, assuming the body is held fixed (i.e. diffraction + Froude–Krylov contributions). In the CFD data it is not measured directly; instead HIVE derives it by subtracting the other modelled force contributions from the measured net hydrodynamic force:

$$
f_e^{CFD}(t) = f_{net}^{CFD}(t) - f_{rad}(t) - f_{hs}(t)
$$

This is stored in the `fe` column of the handled CSV.

---

## Linear excitation force — `fe_lin`

The linear BEM-based excitation force is computed by convolving the wave elevation with the excitation impulse response function $K_e$:

$$
f_e^{lin}(t) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} K_e(\tau)\,\eta(t - \tau)\,d\tau
$$

This is implemented as a convolution in the frequency domain using the FFT and the BEM transfer function $F_e(\omega)$:

$$
f_e^{lin}(t) = \mathcal{F}^{-1}\bigl[F_e(\omega)\,\hat{\eta}(\omega)\bigr]
$$

Stored in the `fe_lin` column. This is the quantity used as the reference input for `linear_si` identification.

---

## Radiation force — `f_rad`

The radiation force arises from waves generated by the body's own motion. In time domain it is expressed as a convolution:

$$
f_{rad}(t) = -\int_0^t K_{rad}(\tau)\,\dot{x}(t-\tau)\,d\tau
$$

where $K_{rad}(\tau)$ is the radiation IRF. For simulation, this integral is replaced by the state-space approximation:

$$
\dot{\mathbf{z}} = A_{rad}\mathbf{z} + B_{rad}\dot{x}, \qquad f_{rad} = C_{rad}\mathbf{z}
$$

The matrices are fitted to $K_{rad}$ using Vector Fitting. Stored in `f_rad`.

---

## Hydrostatic (restoring) force — `f_hs`

The linear hydrostatic restoring force is proportional to displacement:

$$
f_{hs}(t) = -K_{hs}\,x(t), \qquad K_{hs} = \rho g A_{wp}
$$

where $\rho$ is water density, $g$ gravitational acceleration, and $A_{wp}$ the waterplane area. $K_{hs}$ is taken from BEM output. Stored in `f_hs`.

---

## PTO force — `f_pto`

The power take-off force is applied by the WEC control system. For a linear PTO:

$$
f_{pto}(t) = -d_{pto}\,\dot{x}(t) - k_{pto}\,x(t)
$$

where $d_{pto}$ is the PTO damping coefficient and $k_{pto}$ the PTO stiffness (which may be negative, acting as a reactive spring).

These are set via `damping` and `stiffness` in the handle config. Stored in `f_pto`.

---

## Absorbed power — `P_abs`

Instantaneous absorbed power:

$$
P_{abs}(t) = -f_{pto}(t)\,\dot{x}(t)
$$

The sign convention is such that $P_{abs} > 0$ when power is extracted from the wave. Stored in `P_abs`.

---

## Quadratic viscous drag (optional)

When the `linear_viscous` model is used, an additional Morison-type quadratic drag term is included:

$$
f_{visc}(t) = -c_v\,|\dot{x}(t)|\,\dot{x}(t)
$$

The coefficient $c_v$ [N s²/m²] is identified by `run_identify` using the `viscous_drag` scheme.
